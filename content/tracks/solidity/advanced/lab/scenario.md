The market traders on Quayside run a small credit union on chain. Members save ether in a pool that pays them shares, the treasurer adds the interest the union earns elsewhere, and once a month the treasurer signs bonus vouchers that a bonus desk pays into members' savings. In its first month the union lost money four different ways: a contract withdrew the same savings again and again from inside its own payment, a stranger appointed himself treasurer and signed himself a bonus, one bonus voucher was paid out ten times, and an account with no savings at all took ether out in tiny withdrawals. The board has frozen new deposits and hired you to fix the contracts before it reopens. The auditors want every attack they reproduced to fail, and the members want everything else to work exactly as it did.
