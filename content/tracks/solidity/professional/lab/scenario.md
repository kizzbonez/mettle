The pickers' co-operative on a hillside tea estate pays each member's share of the harvest in LEAF, the co-op's token, and most members leave it to build up. The co-op wants a savings vault on chain: members deposit LEAF and receive vault shares; when the estate pays a harvest bonus into the vault, every share is worth more; and when members withdraw, a small exit fee goes to the co-op's treasury to pay for the drying sheds. Thousands of members will keep their savings in it for years, so it must survive an upgrade without anyone's balance moving by a wei, never round in a member's favour at the others' expense, resist the donation attack that robs the first depositors of naive vaults, and stay cheap enough that pickers do not lose their small deposits to gas. Version 2, which the co-op's board has already approved, adds a cap on the total the vault may hold.
